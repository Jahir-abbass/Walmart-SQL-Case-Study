# Walmart Sales Performance Analysis (SQL)

## Objective
Analyze Walmart’s weekly sales data to identify sales trends, store performance, and the impact of holidays on revenue.

## Tools Used
- MySQL
- SQL (Aggregations, Window Functions, Date Functions)

## Key Business Questions
- What is the total revenue across all stores?
- Which stores contribute the most to sales?
- How do holiday weeks impact sales?
- What are the monthly and yearly sales trends?

## Key Insights
- Top-performing stores contribute a large share of total revenue.
- Holiday weeks show higher average sales than non-holiday weeks.
- Sales demonstrate a clear upward trend over time.
- Noticeable seasonality exists in monthly sales.

## Outcome
This project demonstrates the ability to analyze retail sales data and derive business insights using SQL.
